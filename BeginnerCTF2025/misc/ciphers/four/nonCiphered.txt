The world disappeared in a blur,
No sound but a whisper or whirr.
I trudged through the white,
Lost day into night,
Wrapped tight in the hush and the stir.
The cold bit my nose and my toes,
As wind howled and swirled in the snow.
I stumbled and slipped,
On ice I had gripped,
With no clear path forward to go.
I longed for a fire’s warm glow,
But the storm kept its furious flow.
I fought through the freeze,
With trembling knees,
Wondering how long it would grow.
